Responsible Gambling

Fury of Anubis has a 96.52% RTP, which means the house holds an edge of roughly 3.48% on every wager. Over any meaningful number of spins, the mathematically expected outcome for a player is a loss. This is not a criticism of the game — it is how commercial slots are built, and 96.52% is above the industry norm — but it should frame every decision you make about stake size and session length.

Set a loss limit before you start and treat it as fixed. Never increase stakes to recover losses; the odds on the next spin are identical to the last one regardless of what came before. Play only with money whose loss would not affect you, and stop when it stops being entertainment.

If gambling is causing difficulty for you or someone close to you, free confidential support is available from BeGambleAware and GamCare. Most licensed operators also offer deposit limits, time-outs and self-exclusion directly in your account settings.

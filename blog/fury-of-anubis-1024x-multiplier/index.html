Fury of Anubis 1,024× Multiplier Explained

The exact doubling ladder, how bonus starting values change it, and why the multiplier cap is not the same as a 10,000× maximum win.

📅 November 24, 2026✍️ Editorial Team⏱ 7 min read
×2
Core rule: every consecutive winning tumble doubles the active multiplier, up to 1,024×. A new base-game spin resets it to 1×. In Free Spins, each new spin resets to the feature’s current starting multiplier instead.

The complete base-game multiplier ladder

Fury of Anubis uses a 6×5 pays-anywhere grid. After a qualifying win, the winning symbols disappear and new symbols tumble into the open spaces. If those new symbols create another win, the multiplier doubles.

Reaching a stage requires another qualifying win in the same tumble sequence. A multiplier does nothing by itself; it applies only when there is a win to multiply. When the sequence ends, the next base-game spin starts again at 1×.

How Free Spins change the starting point

Free Spins do not begin every spin at 1×. The trigger awards ten spins with a starting multiplier determined by the number of Scatters:

Each free spin resets to that current starting value, then doubles if consecutive wins form. An 8× start can move through 16×, 32×, 64× and beyond within one spin. The next free spin starts back at 8× unless a retrigger has changed the feature’s starting value.

How many doubles reach 1,024×?

From a 1× base-game start, the ladder needs ten increases to show 1,024×. From a 64× free-spin start, four increases produce 128×, 256×, 512× and 1,024×. That shorter visual path does not make the cap guaranteed: each step still needs another win after a tumble.

1,024× multiplier vs 10,000× max win

These figures describe different limits. 1,024× is the cap on the active tumble multiplier. 10,000× is the provider-published ceiling for the total payout relative to the stake. Reaching the multiplier cap does not automatically reach the max win, because the symbol win being multiplied still matters.

The max-win guide covers payout examples and the difference between a ceiling and an unpublished probability.

Can a strategy force a longer tumble chain?

No. Bet timing, spin speed, previous losses and switching between manual and automatic spins do not control the random symbol outcome. Demo mode can teach the sequence, but it cannot forecast the next real-money spin. A chain that stopped at 2× does not make a future 1,024× sequence more likely.

Feature gamble and Bonus Buy

Where available, the pre-feature gamble can risk the awarded feature for a chance to raise its starting multiplier. Bonus Buy can select a guaranteed Scatter tier. Those options change entry conditions, price and variance; they do not guarantee a winning tumble chain or the 1,024× cap. Check the exact in-game rules and local availability before using either.

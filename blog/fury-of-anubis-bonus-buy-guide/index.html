What a higher tier changes

A higher tier raises the starting multiplier for every free spin. It does not add more than ten initial spins, guarantee a winning combination, or remove the need for consecutive winning tumbles. The progressive multiplier doubles only when another win forms after a tumble.

The full feature also includes Scatter retriggers that reset the remaining spin count to ten and can increase the starting multiplier. See the free-spins guide for that sequence.

Direct entry compresses the cost of many spins into one purchase. That can increase the speed and size of losses even though the visual feature lasts longer than one base spin.

Checklist before using a feature purchase

  1. Confirm the feature is legal and offered in your location.
  2. Read the exact price and selected tier before confirming.
  3. Check the in-game RTP and rules for the selected mode.
  4. Set a total session loss limit that includes the full purchase price.
  5. Do not repeat purchases to recover a poor feature.
  6. Never treat a 64x start or 10,000x cap as an expected return.

The lower-risk way to learn the tiers is through a legal free demo. Demo outcomes still do not predict a real-money feature.

How the gamble differs from Bonus Buy

Bonus Buy selects feature entry directly where available. The pre-bonus gamble happens after feature entry has been awarded and risks that entry for a chance to double the starting multiplier. They are separate decisions. Read our bonus gamble guide before confusing an entry tier with a guaranteed multiplier upgrade.

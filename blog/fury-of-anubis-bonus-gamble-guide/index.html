Fury of Anubis Bonus Gamble Guide

What the pre-feature choice changes, what can be lost, and why a larger starting multiplier is not automatically the better decision.

📅 September 10, 2026✍️ Editorial Team⏱ 7 min read
⚡
Short answer: 3-6 Scatters award ten free spins with an 8x-64x starting multiplier. Before the round, the game can offer an optional gamble for a chance to double that starting value—up to 256x. Declining keeps the already-awarded feature.

How feature entry is awarded

Fury of Anubis uses a 6x5 Pays Anywhere grid. Landing three, four, five or six Scatters triggers ten free spins and assigns a progressively larger starting multiplier from 8x to 64x. That multiplier is the floor from which each free spin begins; it is not an instant cash award.

Pragmatic Play publishes these mechanics on its official Fury of Anubis page. Always compare the rules displayed in the exact operator version you open.

What the optional gamble changes

Before the bonus starts, players can accept the awarded starting multiplier or gamble entry to try to double it. The provider describes a possible starting value up to 256x after successful gambles. The attraction is obvious: every winning tumble during the feature begins from a higher multiplier base.

The cost is equally important. A failed gamble can mean losing entry to the feature. It does not merely reduce the multiplier by a small amount. That makes the choice a variance decision—not a method for creating value from a bonus already won.

Why a higher starting multiplier matters

The progressive tumble multiplier resets to the starting value before every free spin. Each consecutive winning tumble then doubles it. Starting at 64x instead of 8x therefore changes the entire progression within a spin—but only when wins and additional tumbles occur. A high starting value does not guarantee a qualifying win.

For the full reset and retrigger sequence, continue to our Fury of Anubis free-spins guide.

How to make the decision responsibly

  • Decide before play whether you will ever use a gamble feature.
  • Treat the value of feature entry as already at risk if you press gamble.
  • Do not gamble because previous attempts lost or because a success feels “due.”
  • Do not increase subsequent stakes to recover a failed feature gamble.
  • Use demo mode to learn the interface without wagering money.

No past result changes the probability of the next independent outcome. If losing the awarded bonus would cause frustration or chasing, the lowest-risk decision is to decline the gamble.

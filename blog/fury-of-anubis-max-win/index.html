Fury of Anubis Max Win: What 10,000× Actually Means

Published 1 October 2026 · FuryOfAnubis.casino Editorial Team

Pragmatic Play publishes a 10,000× maximum win for Fury of Anubis. That figure is the game's total payout ceiling relative to the total bet. It is not the same as the multiplier displayed above the grid, and it does not mean that a 10,000× result has a known or likely frequency.

Max win and max multiplier are different

Fury of Anubis uses a 6×5 pays-anywhere grid. Eight or more matching symbols create a win, those symbols tumble away, and the multiplier doubles after consecutive winning tumbles. In ordinary base play, it starts at 1× and can rise to 1,024× before resetting when that spin ends.

The 1,024× figure modifies a qualifying win. The 10,000× figure caps the total win relative to the bet. A large multiplier without a qualifying symbol win does not create a payout, and a 1,024× multiplier should not be read as a guarantee of a 1,024× return.

How Free Spins change the starting multiplier

Three to six Scatters trigger ten Free Spins with a starting multiplier from 8× to 64×. The tumble multiplier works during the feature, but it resets to the awarded starting value before each new Free Spin rather than returning to 1×.

Before the bonus begins, an optional gamble can risk feature entry for a chance to double the starting multiplier, up to 256×. During Free Spins, three or more Scatters reset the remaining spin count to ten and can multiply the starting value. These mechanics increase the possible multiplier path; none guarantees a particular payout.

For the complete progression, use the Free Spins guide. The bonus gamble guide explains the risk of forfeiting feature entry.

Do Super Spins remove the 10,000× cap?

Pragmatic Play says selected markets may offer a mode that starts at 256× and removes the maximum limit from the tumble multiplier. That wording concerns the multiplier's progression, not the published total-win ceiling.

Do not assume an unlimited multiplier means an unlimited payout. Check the exact game rules and confirmation screen before selecting any enhanced bet. Our special-bets guide separates Super Spins from Bonus Buy and the pre-feature gamble.

What are the odds of hitting the maximum?

The public Pragmatic Play game, campaign and launch pages reviewed for this article do not publish a “one in X” probability for a 10,000× result. We therefore do not quote one. A number copied from another affiliate page or inferred from a short demo session would not be a reliable primary-source statistic.

Can the free demo estimate max-win frequency?

No. The free demo is useful for learning the controls, tumble sequence, Scatter awards and multiplier resets. Even thousands of personal spins would be too small and uncontrolled a sample to establish the probability of a rare maximum result.

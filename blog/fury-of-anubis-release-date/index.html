When Did Fury of Anubis Come Out?

Fury of Anubis was released on 25 June 2026 by Pragmatic Play. It arrived as part of the studio’s mid-year rollout, joining an already dense Egyptian-themed catalogue but landing with a mechanic set closer to the Gates of Olympus and Sweet Bonanza lineage than to traditional Egyptian slots.

The game went live simultaneously across most Pragmatic Play-licensed operators, with the free demo appearing on the studio’s public demo server the same day. That simultaneous launch is standard practice for Pragmatic and is why the slot appeared on dozens of casino lobbies within hours rather than rolling out gradually.

What Made the Launch Notable

The progressive multiplier was the clearest launch differentiator. It starts at ×1 in the base game and doubles on every consecutive winning tumble, reaching a published ceiling of ×1,024 before resetting for the next spin.

The bonus design also arrived with clearly documented starting values: 3–6 scatters award 10 free spins with an initial multiplier from ×8 to ×64, plus a pre-feature gamble that can raise it to ×256. Those mechanics are confirmed in the provider’s launch announcement.

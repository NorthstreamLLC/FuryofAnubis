Fury of Anubis Special Bets: Super Spins vs Bonus Buy

Published 16 September 2026 · Editorial guide

Super Spins, Bonus Buy and the pre-bonus gamble change different things. Understanding the distinction matters more than choosing the largest multiplier. None guarantees a profit.

Which option does what?

  • Ordinary spins play the base game and wait for a natural feature trigger.
  • Super Spins change starting-multiplier behaviour.
  • Bonus Buy purchases entry into a free-spin tier.
  • The pre-bonus gamble risks feature entry for a chance at a higher starting multiplier.

Special bets are offered only in selected markets. Check your game's information screen and confirmation dialog for current availability, prices and rules.

The published Super Spin modes

Pragmatic Play describes one mode that randomly resets the starting tumble multiplier to 2x–1,024x each spin. Another starts at 256x and removes the tumble multiplier's maximum limit.

A multiplier limit and the game's payout cap are different: the publisher lists a maximum win of 10,000x. A starting multiplier is not a payout by itself; a qualifying win is still needed.

A large multiplier can distract from the cost of each attempt. Read our multiplier guide for the difference between a multiplier and actual money returned.

Bonus Buy is feature entry

The four published tiers guarantee three, four, five or six scatters. Each awards ten free spins, starting at 8x, 16x, 32x or 64x respectively. Those are entry conditions, not guaranteed returns.

Our Bonus Buy tier guide compares the tiers. The free-spins guide explains what happens inside the feature. A purchase concentrates spending into one event rather than spreading it across ordinary spins.

The gamble risks an existing feature

The publisher describes a gamble before entry that can increase the starting multiplier up to 256x while risking the free-spin feature. Losing can forfeit entry.

Do not assume a gamble button is a required step. Read the displayed alternatives if you want to keep the feature already triggered. See the bonus gamble explanation.

Frequently asked questions

Is Super Spin the same as buying free spins?

No. It changes multiplier behaviour; Bonus Buy purchases feature entry. Read the mode description instead of relying on button placement.

Does a bigger starting multiplier guarantee a bigger win?

No. Winning symbols, feature outcomes and purchase cost still matter.

Why is a mode missing?

Availability varies by market and offering. Do not bypass regional restrictions.

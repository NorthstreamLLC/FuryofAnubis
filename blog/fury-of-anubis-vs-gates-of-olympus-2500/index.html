The multiplier systems are fundamentally different

Fury of Anubis starts its base-game tumble multiplier at 1×. Every consecutive winning tumble doubles it—2×, 4×, 8× and onward—up to 1,024×. A new paid spin resets the base-game sequence. During Free Spins, a selected starting multiplier applies at the beginning of each spin before any successful tumbles increase it.

Gates of Olympus 2500 instead lands multiplier symbols with values up to 2,500×. Its Bonus Wheel selects one of two multiplier rules or a Super version of either rule. In one mode multipliers apply to the current winning sequence; in the persistent mode, collected multiplier values also affect later Free Spins. The Super modes raise the minimum multiplier value.

The practical choice is therefore not simply 1,024 versus 2,500. Fury rewards a chain of consecutive winning tumbles through exponential doubling. Gates 2500 rewards multiplier-symbol landings under whichever bonus rule the wheel selects.

Free Spins entry and feature structure

Fury of Anubis presents multiple feature-entry tiers tied to different starting multipliers, plus an optional pre-feature gamble in supported versions. The selected starting value changes where each Free Spin begins, but every individual spin still depends on winning tumbles to climb.

Gates of Olympus 2500 awards 15 Free Spins from four or more scatters. Three or more scatters during the feature add five spins. Before the round, a Bonus Wheel chooses Free Spins 1, Free Spins 2, Super Free Spins 1 or Super Free Spins 2. That random mode selection is a defining part of Gates 2500’s bonus design.

Maximum win and multiplier ceiling

Gates of Olympus 2500 has the larger published maximum win at 25,000×, compared with 10,000× for Fury of Anubis. These are payout ceilings, not typical bonus results or probabilities. A larger maximum does not mean a game is more likely to return a profit.

Fury’s 1,024× figure is the top progressive multiplier, while Gates’ 2,500× is the highest individual multiplier symbol. Neither number should be treated as a standalone payout: the underlying winning symbols and the feature rules determine what the multiplier applies to.

RTP and volatility

The top published configuration for both games is 96.52%. Both also have lower operator configurations, which is why players should check the in-game panel rather than relying on a search snippet or another casino’s screenshot.

Pragmatic Play describes Gates of Olympus 2500 as high volatility. Fury of Anubis can also produce wide short-term swings, but we do not assign an invented numerical volatility rating when the public provider material does not publish one. RTP and volatility describe different things: RTP concerns theoretical long-run return, while volatility describes how unevenly outcomes can be distributed.

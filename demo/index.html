What You’ll See in Your First Demo Session

Fury of Anubis is a scatter-pay slot, which means paylines do not exist. Symbols pay wherever they land as long as at least eight of the same type appear anywhere on the 5×6 grid. If you have only played line-based slots before, the first few demo spins can look confusing — wins seem to appear from nowhere. They are not random bugs; the game is simply counting symbols across the whole screen rather than along fixed lines.

Once a win lands, the winning symbols are removed and new ones tumble down to fill the gaps. If that creates another win, the process repeats. The multiplier counter above the reels starts every spin at ×1 and doubles with each consecutive tumble — ×2, ×4, ×8, and onward to a ceiling of ×1,024. Long tumble chains are where the meaningful wins in this game come from, not from single large symbol hits.

At a base hit frequency of 1 in 3.59, you should expect roughly one winning spin in every three or four. That is a comparatively generous rate, and it is why the game is rated medium volatility (3/5) rather than high. Your demo balance will move constantly, but most of those wins will be small. The bonus round is where the game does its real work.

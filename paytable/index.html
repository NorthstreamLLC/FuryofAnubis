📊 Reading the Paytable Correctly

Every value on this page is a bet multiplier applied before the tumble multiplier. That distinction is where most players misread scatter-pay slots. A payout of 100× for twelve Golden Scarabs is the base figure. If that win arrives on the fourth consecutive tumble of a base-game spin, the multiplier counter is already at ×8, so the actual credit is 800× your stake. The paytable is the starting point of the calculation, never the end of it.

The second thing worth internalising is the steepness of the tiers. Golden Scarab pays 5× at eight symbols and 100× at twelve — a twentyfold jump for four extra symbols. Every symbol in the game follows this shape. It means the difference between a mediocre spin and a memorable one is usually just two or three additional matching symbols, which is exactly what tumbling reels are designed to manufacture.

Low-pay gems are almost irrelevant on their own. Eight Cyan Gems return 0.2× — a fifth of your stake on a winning spin. Their function is not to pay you; it is to clear space on the grid so the tumble chain continues and the multiplier keeps doubling. Treat small gem wins as progress toward a multiplier, not as wins.

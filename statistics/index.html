How to Read the RTP

The 96.52% RTP represents the theoretical long-term return across millions of spins. It does not mean you'll get $96.52 back for every $100 in a single session — short-term variance can deviate significantly in either direction.

ℹ️
What is not published: Pragmatic Play’s public materials do not provide a base-game/bonus RTP split, exact hit frequency, natural bonus frequency or maximum-win probability for Fury of Anubis. We therefore do not label third-party estimates as official data. Check the information screen in the version you are playing for its displayed rules and RTP.

Variance: What the Mechanics Suggest

Pragmatic Play’s public page does not assign Fury of Anubis a numerical volatility rating. The 10,000× ceiling, escalating multipliers and selectable feature options can still produce wide short-term swings, so bankroll claims should not be inferred from an unofficial label.

What Is Known
Wins tumble, and each consecutive winning tumble doubles the multiplier. The bonus starts with 10 free spins and an 8×–64× multiplier, depending on the number of scatters.
What Is Not Known Publicly
An exact hit rate, bonus rate and payout distribution are not stated in the provider’s public materials. Session length and expected dry spells cannot be responsibly predicted from RTP alone.
